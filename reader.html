The town had been quiet for three days.

Snow covered the narrow roads, the rooftops, and the old trees surrounding the town. Nothing moved beneath the pale winter sky.

People had grown accustomed to the silence. They had lived with it for years.

But that morning, something was different.

Nobody knew exactly when the strange feeling had begun. There was no sound to point toward, no shadow standing at the end of the street.

Only the quiet.

And somewhere beyond the houses, beneath the snow, something waited to be found.
