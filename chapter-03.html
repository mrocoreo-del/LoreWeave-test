RECORD 001

The Stranger

CHAPTER 03

Min-jun had never seen the man before.

He stood at the end of the road beneath an old black umbrella, completely still while the snow gathered around his shoes.

The strange thing was that nobody else seemed to notice him.

Min-jun looked toward the houses.

Curtains moved behind several windows. Someone was watching the storm.

When he looked back, the stranger was gone.

Min-jun frowned.

Then he noticed something in the snow.

Footprints.

They began in the middle of the road.

And they ended directly in front of him.
