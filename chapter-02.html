RECORD 001

The Snow

CHAPTER 02

By morning, the town had disappeared beneath the snow.

Roofs became white shapes. Roads vanished beneath layers of ice. Even the old wooden fences looked as if they had been swallowed overnight.

Nobody complained.

Winter had always been like this.

But that morning, something felt different.

The forest at the edge of town was completely silent. No birds. No branches moving in the wind. Nothing.

Min-jun stopped walking.

He stared toward the trees.

For a moment, he thought he saw something moving between them.

Then the snow began falling harder.
